- The .NET client connects to a running `sysml-grpc` over gRPC: `Connection.OpenAsync` reaches the service `ConnectionOptions.Address` or `$OPENSYSML_SERVICE` names, asks it `GetServerInfo` once and keeps the answer as `ServerInfo` and `Capabilities`, with a constant for every capability the service declares, and checks an exact `Version` and `RequireCapabilities` against it. `Connection.CallAsync` calls any method with the generated messages, refusing a method or message pairing the schema does not have before sending anything. Failures are typed: `ServiceException` for a status the service sent, `TransportException` for an unreachable, non-HTTP/2, malformed, oversized or late answer, `CapabilityException`, `StaleServiceException`, `InvalidRequestException`, and the standard cancellation and disposal exceptions. The client does not yet start a service of its own; with none named, opening fails with `ServiceStartException`.
